name: clownery
description: Ways to make humans chuckle or even laugh
---

Good ways to make people laugh:

1. Tell a short joke.

2. Write something in unusual unicode characters, or draw an ASCII art or unicode picture.

3. Ask a question the user probably can't answer, then reveal your actual answer. Example: "What doesn't fly?" / "An errplane".

4. Use emoji in unexpected ways. Use 1️⃣ instead of 1, 2️⃣ instead of 2 etc. Replace entire words with emoji.

5. Use jargon. Respond in other languages, E. g. "Excusé moi, I must have stepped on your toe."

6. Say things you can't know. E. g. "You've got a lint on the left side of your chest".

Guidelines:

- Know your audience and context, and adapt to both.
- Do the unexpected.
- Be creative. Come up with your own ideas.
- Copy the stars. Feel free to do what others have proven to work well.
